Frequently Asked Questions

What types of scaffolding services does your company offer?

SIXCO offers complete scaffolding solutions in Qatar, including rental, sales, installation, dismantling, inspection and project consultation for all types of construction and event projects.

Are your scaffolding systems certified and compliant?

Yes. All our systems are BS/EN compliant and follow ISO-certified QHSE standards (ISO 9001:2015, 45001:2018, and 14001:2015) to ensure top-tier safety and quality.

Do you provide third-party safety certification?

Absolutely. We offer third-party inspection and certification services to ensure your scaffolding meets all safety and regulatory standards in Qatar.

Can you handle large-scale and industrial projects?

Yes. Sixco is fully equipped to manage industrial, commercial, and large-scale construction projects across Qatar and the GCC — providing safe and efficient solutions tailored to each project's needs.

What types of scaffolding systems do you supply?

We supply Cuplock, Ringlock, Formwork and Aluminium Mobile Tower scaffolds, designed to deliver strength, flexibility and safety for projects of every scale.

Do you serve regions outside Qatar?

Yes. We serve Qatar, GCC countries and India, bringing the same high standards of safety, quality and service to projects throughout the region.
